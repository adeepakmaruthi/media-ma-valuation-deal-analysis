# Investment Banking-Style Presentation Outline
**Hypothetical media-sector M&A case inspired by Paramount / Warner Bros. Discovery strategic themes. All figures are fictional.**

1. **Deal thesis:** strategic rationale, transaction question and caveat that this is a hypothetical case.
2. **Buyer profile:** fictional diversified media business, revenue streams and operating challenges.
3. **Target profile:** fictional studio, streaming platform, content library and network assets.
4. **Industry dynamics:** streaming profitability, linear-TV decline, advertising, sports rights and content monetisation.
5. **Historical financials:** illustrative revenue, EBITDA, net debt and profitability trends.
6. **Forecast assumptions:** revenue growth, EBITDA margin, content amortisation, capex and working capital.
7. **DCF valuation:** CAPM, WACC, FCFF, terminal value and enterprise-to-equity bridge.
8. **Trading comparables:** fictional EV/EBITDA peers, valuation cross-check and peer limitations.
9. **Offer and transaction structure:** unaffected equity value, premium, debt refinancing and transaction fees.
10. **Sources & uses:** acquisition debt, stock consideration and buyer cash; confirm sources equal uses.
11. **EPS and synergies:** run-rate synergies, realization, dis-synergies, integration costs, financing costs and pro forma EPS.
12. **Sensitivity / risks / conclusion:** WACC-growth matrix, synergy-debt matrix, regulatory review, leverage, content and execution risks; conclude conditionally.
